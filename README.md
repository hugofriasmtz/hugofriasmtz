# 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm Hugo, a developer with professional experience and a particular taste for everything that happens behind the scenes in a system.

I've always been driven by curiosity: understanding how things work from the inside and breaking down complex problems into simple steps. I firmly believe that good software isn't just what compiles, but what is well thought out, well documented, and made with those who will use or maintain it in mind.

Currently, I am open to new challenges, both in **full-time openings and freelance projects**.

Outside of code, you can find me playing football ⚽, hanging out with friends, listening to music in my free time 🎧, or simply sharing good moments away from the screen.
